About

Md. Omaer Faruq Goni is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh. He received his M.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering (Dec 2023) and B.Sc. in Electrical & Computer Engineering (Feb 2021) from RUET. His research spans Deep Learning, Medical Image Analysis, Power Systems, Computer Architecture, Robotics, and IoT. He has authored multiple peer-reviewed journal and conference papers and contributes to open-source projects and student supervision. He is available for PhD student applications and research collaborations.
